Data Subject Request Portal

This portal lets you submit a request related to your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Articles 15–22.

You can request:

  • Access (Art 15) — a copy of your data we hold
  • Rectification (Art 16) — correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art 17) — deletion of your data
  • Restriction (Art 18) — pause processing
  • Portability (Art 20) — machine-readable export
  • Objection (Art 21) — object to processing
  • Automated decision review (Art 22)
